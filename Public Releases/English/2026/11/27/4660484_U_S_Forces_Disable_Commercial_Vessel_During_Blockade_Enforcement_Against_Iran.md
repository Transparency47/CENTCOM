# U.S. Forces Disable Commercial Vessel During Blockade Enforcement Against Iran

TAMPA, Fla. – U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) disabled a commercial vessel in the Gulf of Oman, Nov. 26, after the crew attempted to run the U.S. naval blockade, despite multiple warnings, following departure from an Iranian port.

A U.S. military aircraft launched precision munitions that struck the stern of Palau-flagged M/V Pacific Archer. The ship’s propulsion was ultimately disabled and the civilian crew was unharmed.

The bulk carrier attempted to transit regional waters after departing Bandar Abbas.

CENTCOM continues to strictly enforce the blockade. U.S. forces have redirected 158 compliant commercial vessels and disabled six that refused to comply.

All mariners operating in the Gulf of Oman and approaches to the Strait of Hormuz are urged to heed blockade warnings issued by CENTCOM forces.
